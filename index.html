What is Healthy?

Health checks let your infrastructure know your site is alive and ready. WordPress doesn't provide them natively, but Healthy does!

Healthy provides stable Health Check endpoints. Works with uptime monitors, Kubernetes readiness probes, and any system that speaks HTTP(S).

All features Documentation

Get Healthy

Download Healthy and keep your WordPress site in perfect shape.

  1. Download the plugin
  2. Extract and upload it to /wp-content/plugins/
  3. Activate it in the WordPress admin
  4. Find Healthy in the Settings menu and get the monitoring endpoint
