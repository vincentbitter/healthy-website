We're excited to introduce the new Healthy website! A fresh home for everything related to the Healthy WordPress plugin.

This first version is intentionally simple, but it already gives us a solid foundation to build on. Over the past days we've been shaping the structure, refining the layout, and creating a clean design system that will support all future content. From the hero section to the news grid, from the split "What is Healthy" layout component on the frontpage to the improved navigation. The site is now ready to grow with Healthy itself.

There's still plenty to add: detailed feature pages, documentation, screenshots, examples, and more. But the essentials are in place. A clear structure, a modern look, and a fast, lightweight setup powered by Jekyll.

In the coming weeks we'll continue expanding the site with new content and improvements. For now, we're happy to share this first milestone with you. Thanks for following along and welcome to the new Healthy website.
